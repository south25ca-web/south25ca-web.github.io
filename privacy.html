Privacy Policy

This application is used privately by its owner to synchronize files between a personal computer and the owner's Google Drive account using rclone.

Data Access

The application accesses Google Drive files only as authorized by the account owner.

Data Use

Google Drive data is used solely for personal file synchronization and backup.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share Google Drive data with third parties.

Data Storage

Files are stored on the owner's computer and Google Drive. Authentication credentials are stored locally by rclone.

Access Revocation

The owner may revoke access through their Google Account security settings.
